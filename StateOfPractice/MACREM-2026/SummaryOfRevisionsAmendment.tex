\item 3.7 For the change to the data collection methods, please address the
following.  ``Please describe the platform you will use to collect survey data,
and describe any collection, storage, and deletion procedures for this new kind
of data you are collecting. Make sure this is reflected in the LOI and survey

\emph{The response to 3.7 has been modified to address the reviewer's concerns,
with the following additional text: The survey is hosted on Microsoft forms
through McMaster's Microsoft 365.  The survey is available
\href{https://forms.cloud.microsoft/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=B2M3RCm0rUKMJSjNSW9HcijtxlD0eP9Et28dpBjT1etURFNCTFFWREhCMEo4NUJESVdKQ04zRUxGOS4u&route=shorturl}

There is no survey question to collect the participant's name.  However, the
graduate students will know the set of people who were invited, and the principal
investigator will know what raw data is associated with which project.  Although
the identity of the participants are not made explicit, it would be possible to
deduce a small set of possibilities from this available information.  The LOI
and survey consent preamble have been modified to make participants aware of this.
The survey response data will be stored under Ziyang Fang's Microsoft 365
account.  Only the two graduate students (Fang and Schankula) and the principal
investigator (Smith) will have access to the data through their McMaster ID and
authentication.
At the conclusion of the retention period approved for this study, the
researcher will delete the raw survey data by:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Deleting all responses from the Microsoft Form.
\item Deleting the associated Excel workbook containing the responses from OneDrive.
\item Removing the deleted items from the Microsoft 365 Recycle Bin and, if accessible, the Second-Stage Recycle Bin.
\end{enumerate}
Following these steps, the data will no longer be accessible to the research
team through Microsoft 365. Residual copies may persist temporarily within
McMaster University's managed Microsoft 365 backup and retention infrastructure
in accordance with institutional policies, after which they are managed and
disposed of by the institution and Microsoft according to their retention
schedules. As for the interview transcript data, the raw survey data will be
retained for 3 years after the study.}
